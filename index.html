Our Story

Majan Cooling Co. was founded in 1996 as a small air conditioning service company, initially serving limited areas in Darsait and Ruwi, Oman. What started as a humble operation with a passion for excellence has grown into a trusted name across the entire Sultanate of Oman and now extends to the United Arab Emirates.

Over the years, our dedication to quality and customer satisfaction has driven our remarkable growth. Today, we proudly serve 200+ satisfied customers with our comprehensive AC solutions. Our expansion from a neighborhood service provider to serving almost all of Oman and now the UAE reflects our commitment to delivering exceptional cooling solutions across the Gulf region.

What makes us unique is our unwavering commitment to premium quality. We never cut corners. Instead, we exclusively partner with the best brands and use only top-tier components for every installation and repair. Our team of highly skilled technicians ensures that every job is completed with utmost care and responsibility.

Most importantly, we deliver all these premium services at affordable prices, making quality AC solutions accessible to everyone. This is our promise to every customer—exceptional quality without breaking the bank.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide the best air conditioning services at affordable prices, ensuring that premium cooling solutions are accessible to every customer in Oman and the UAE.
